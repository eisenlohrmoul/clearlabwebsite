For medical students

Medical student opportunities in the CLEAR Lab

We study why the menstrual cycle shapes mood, substance use, and suicide risk in some people and not others. Medical students have contributed to this work from the beginning.

How students get involved

Most students begin by helping with data collection in our clinical trials, which is the best way to learn what the measurements mean, and go on to a scholarly project using data from the lab’s completed studies. The arrangement is flexible; a student with strong analytic skills, for example, may work on data analysis from the start.

1 · Data collection

Help run our clinical trials.

Participant visits, structured clinical interviews, daily safety check-in calls, biospecimen collection, and fMRI sessions. Our next trial, PINES, pairs perimenstrual estradiol with resting-state fMRI.

A small, regular commitment is enough to begin.

2 · Scholarly projects

Analyze data from the lab’s completed studies.

Daily symptom and hormone data together with neuroimaging, alongside senior MD/PhD students. Projects routinely lead to conference posters and coauthored papers.

Suited to a research requirement, a summer, or a research year.
