CLEAR
Clarifying the Endocrinology of Acute Risk
University of Chicago · Department of Psychiatry & Behavioral Neuroscience

The CLEAR Lab studies brain sensitivity to hormones across the menstrual cycle.

Hormone sensitivity is an unusually strong brain response to ordinary hormone shifts, and it can drive severe depression, irritability, suicide risk, and harmful substance use. We measure and manipulate the menstrual cycle to identify and treat these sensitivities.

From mechanism to treatment

1 · Framework development

Develop personalized models of cycle sensitivity.

The DASH-MC framework, Dimensional Affective Sensitivity to Hormones, maps why some people are sensitive to estradiol (E2), progesterone (P4), or both, while most are unaffected. We think about hormone sensitivity across disorders, not only in PMDD.

E2 surgeP4/ALLO surgeE2 drop

Peters et al., Molecular Psychiatry, 2025dashmc.base44.app

2 · Measurement & modeling

Build better tools to study the menstrual cycle.

Our phase-aligned cycle time scaling (PACTS) approach, implemented in our open-source menstrualcycleR R package, realigns hormonal cycles across patients and months. Generalized additive models can be used to model average, subgroup, and person-specific cycle effects.

Nagpal et al., PNEC, 2025menstrualcycler.base44.app

3 · Clinical trials

Test the mechanisms of different cycle sensitivities.

We pair randomized crossover hormone trials with real-time symptom reports, neuroimaging, and passive smartphone data to isolate how estradiol (E2) and progesterone (P4) each drive risk, revealing distinct biological pathways we can target.

Eisenlohr-Moul et al., Transl Psych, 2022Upcoming trial: the PINES study
